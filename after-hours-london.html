About the event

A pre-party built for the Sifted Summit crowd.

After Hours: London kicks off the night before Sifted Summit begins, bringing together founders, operators, and investors in Shoreditch for an evening of cocktails and conversation. The night centers on a founder fireside chat, followed by open networking in a relaxed, high-end setting.

As a sponsor, your brand is woven into the evening itself: signage and branding on-site, a moment in front of the room, and direct face time with attendees who don't usually take a cold pitch.
