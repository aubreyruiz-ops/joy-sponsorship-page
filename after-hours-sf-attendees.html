The forecast

Is 2027 a good year to raise?

Every founder planning a raise is asking the same thing. If the groundhog sees its shadow, it’s six more weeks of winter; if it doesn’t, spring comes early. Fundraising has its own version of that forecast, and “funding winter” is already how founders and investors describe a tight market.

Early February is when many investors have set their plans for the year and founders are gearing up to raise, so it’s the right moment to ask which season 2027 will be.

If the groundhog sees its shadow

A long winter

Capital stays cautious and the bar keeps rising.

  • Rounds stay smaller and take longer to close
  • Valuations stay flat or slip while investors ask for more proof
  • Funding concentrates in a handful of sectors
If it doesn’t

An early spring

Confidence returns and capital starts moving again.

  • Rounds get bigger and close faster
  • Valuations begin to recover as competition returns
  • Investors widen their search to new sectors

Which will 2027 be? That’s the question for the night.
