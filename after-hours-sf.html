About the event

An evening built for the Bay Area founder crowd.

After Hours: San Francisco brings together founders, operators, and investors at Shack15, the Ferry Building's community hub for entrepreneurs, for an evening of cocktails and conversation. Fundraising usually gets talked about after the fact, so we wanted the real conversation: a founder and an investor on stage for an honest fireside chat about how a round came together, what investors look for, and what each side wishes they'd known. Open networking follows in a relaxed, high-end setting.

Joy plans corporate events year-round, and After Hours is where that work gets personal: a small, curated room where the brands backing the night meet the founders, operators, and investors they want to know. As a sponsor, your brand is woven into the evening itself: signage and branding on-site, a moment in front of the room, and direct face time with attendees who don't usually take a cold pitch.
